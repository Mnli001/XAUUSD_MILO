# XAU Analytics app shell and Today

Scope: whole app redesign (landing, auth, Today, Chart, News, Setup, Journal, Performance, Bot, Settings, Admin). Mode: Operate for app and admin; the landing page is Persuade inside the same world.

Audience and task: Mongolian retail gold traders, phone first, mostly in the evening and night Ulaanbaatar time (London 15:00–00:00, New York 21:00–05:00). Decide side, timing and news risk in under a minute; log and review trades.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The trading day read as a departure timetable. Sessions are coloured lines on one true-scale 24-hour axis, hours are departures with up/down odds, news are scheduled arrivals with countdowns. Refuses the category default of a black-and-gold card dashboard with a gauge.

OWN-WORLD: Night-platform blue-black ground (#0d1720), raised enamel panels (#16232e), enamel white text (#eef2ee). Session line colours: Asia violet #8b6cf0, London blue #2f7de1, New York yellow #f2b632. State: up signal green #1fb57a, down vermilion #ef5a3c, always paired with arrow or word. Geologica for everything, tabular numerals right-aligned. Thin rules, 6px line strokes with round caps, small round line badges. Light theme: daylight platform (pale blue-grey enamel) with the same lines deepened.

STORY: The trader sees where in the day they are, which side dominates and how strongly, which hours ahead lean up or down, and when the next news arrives; then opens Chart, Setup, Journal or Bot from the same line vocabulary.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Phone: top bar with name and live price. Under it the day line: 24h axis, three session strokes at true length, a white "now" marker. Then the dominant side as the largest type on screen with the four timeframe scores. Then the hourly timetable rows (hour, session colour badge, session name, up %, down %), current hour highlighted. Next high-impact news as a yellow arrival strip with a countdown. Bottom tab bar. Desktop: left rail nav, the same blocks in two columns.

FORM: Departure timetable / UB rail line diagram, position 3 of 7 on my ordered list. seed:fed9e0180ac7c3e1. Raises: true-length time spans (labanotation), one dominant answer per screen (zoo map), tabular right-aligned columns (datamatics), whole-cell hour grid (doujin catalog).

Signature move: the session line diagram, reused as the page header on Today, the hour axis on News, and the session key on Chart and Setup.
