# XAU Analytics app shell and Today

Scope: whole app (landing, auth, Today, Chart, News, Setup, Journal, Performance, Bot, Settings, Admin). Mode: Operate for app and admin; the landing page is Persuade inside the same world. This replaces the earlier departure-timetable world (user asked for a more luxurious, higher-quality look).

Audience and task: Mongolian retail gold traders, phone first, evenings and nights Ulaanbaatar time. Decide side, timing and news risk in under a minute; log and review trades; run the MT5 robot.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every figure struck like the assay hallmark on a gold bar. The day's answer is a stamped cartouche (purity-style score, serial date), not a card with a gauge. Refuses glossy black-and-gold gradients and gold text effects.

OWN-WORLD: Vault ground, a deep green-black (#0a1410 family) with raised vault panels; cast gold (#d6b25e matte, never a gradient) for hallmarks, rules and the brand stamp; platinum white text; up in bright jade-mint, down in vermilion, always with a drawn arrow or word. Sessions as metals: Asia rose-copper, London platinum, New York fine gold. Unbounded (wide, stamped capitals) for hallmarks and display; Geologica for UI with tabular numerals. Fine engraved hairline double rules frame the hallmark.

STORY: The trader reads the hallmark (side and strength), sees where the day stands on the session line, which hours lean which way, and when the next release lands; then moves to Chart, Setup, Journal or Bot in the same vault.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Phone: top bar with the hallmark brand stamp. Under it a compact price line, the session line, then the day's hallmark cartouche (ӨСӨХ / УНАХ in stamped capitals, score, date serial, four timeframe marks) as the largest object; the next-release strip with countdown; then hour rows. Desktop: left rail, the same blocks in two columns.
